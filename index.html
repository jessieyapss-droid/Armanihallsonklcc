07Before you ask

What people ask first.

What's the price?

It depends on layout, floor and facing, and packages are updated from time to time. WhatsApp me and I'll send you the current figure for the layouts you're interested in.

Can I really walk to KLCC?

Yes. According to the developer, the project has an exclusive covered walkway connecting to KLCC, about 300 metres away. The final design of the link is subject to authorities' approval.

Is it really freehold?

Yes. The land tenure is freehold.

When will it be completed?

Completion is expected in 2029.

How much is the monthly maintenance?

RM0.95 psf, sinking fund included. A 406 sq ft unit works out to roughly RM386 a month. Every layout above has its figure worked out.

Does it come with parking?

The development has 1,189 parking lots across levels 1 to 9. Allocation differs by unit type — I'll take you through exactly what applies to your layout at the viewing.

Do I have to pay or commit to anything to view?

No. The viewing is free and carries no obligation. Just bring your questions.

I'm outstation or overseas. Can I still view?

Yes. WhatsApp me and we can arrange a video walkthrough, with the material sent to you directly.
